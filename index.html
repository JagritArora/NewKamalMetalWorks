Operations

Four operations.
One continuous line.

Follow a single blank from coil to finished coat — blanked, punched, bent and plated, in the order the part actually moves across the floor.

01 / 04

Blanking

A steel strip feeds onto the line. Press 01 shears the outer profile from the sheet in a single clean stroke.

02 / 04

Punching

Press 02 pierces every hole and slot in one hit. The offcut slugs drop clear through the die.

03 / 04

Bending

Press 03 forms the centre band down and rotates both flanges up in one linked stroke. The part leaves the die at its final angle.

04 / 04

Plating

A fine powder builds an even coat across every face, then a final pass brings the part up to a uniform satin finish.

Capabilities

Everything else
the part needs.

Embossing, threading, notching and knurling — added to the same job, so a part leaves here finished rather than half made.

Embossing
01 / 04

Embossing

A raised boss or bead is formed into the sheet in one stroke — stiffening a panel or fixing a locating point without removing any material.

Threading
02 / 04

Threading

Holes are tapped to spec, so the fastener drives home straight and your line skips an assembly step altogether.

Notching
03 / 04

Notching

Corners and edges are relieved in a single hit, so the part folds clean and mates without interference downstream.

Knurling
04 / 04

Knurling

A diamond or straight pattern is rolled into the surface — a sure hand-grip, or a press-fit that won't spin loose.

Tooling

A part is only as good
as the tool behind it.

Every component starts as a die, not a sheet. We design, build and maintain our own progressive and compound tools in-house — so tolerance, cycle time and tool life are settled long before your first production run.

In-house die design
& build

Tools are engineered and built on-site, station by station, before the first sheet ever runs.

Progressive &
compound tooling

One strip, many stations, a single pass — fewer handlings per part, and lower cost at volume.

Tool maintenance
& repair

Dies are serviced and re-ground in-house, so tool wear never becomes your delivery problem.

First-article
approval

Every new tool proves itself on a first-article sample before a full run is cleared.

Quality

Checked at every station,
not just at the end.

Inspection is built into the run, not bolted on at the end — and the whole system is certified to ISO 9001:2015.

Incoming material checks

Coil and sheet stock verified against mill test certificates before it's released to the floor.

First-article & in-process inspection

Every new tool is sampled and measured before a full run starts, then re-checked at intervals through it.

Calibrated gauging

Dimensions confirmed with calibrated plug, ring and fixture gauges at the press, not only at final QC.

Final inspection & traceability
